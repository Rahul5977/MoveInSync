# Kestrel Bank — Employee Commute Policy (Version 1, effective 2026-02-15)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy governs company-provided commute services for employees of Kestrel Bank at the Mumbai (Bandra Kurla Complex) and Chennai (Guindy) operations centres. It applies to permanent employees of all grades. Employees of outsourced service partners are covered by their own employer's arrangements and not by this policy. Commute services are delivered through the MoveInSync platform and administered by the Transport Administration team.

## 2. Definitions
**Transport Admin** means the Transport Administration team responsible for rosters, vendors and approvals. **Operations shift** means any shift starting before 7:00 AM or ending after 8:00 PM. **Pickup** is a trip from home to office; **drop** is a trip from office to home. **Ad-hoc trip** means a trip that is not part of the published roster. **No-show** means failure to board a rostered cab within the wait time without a prior cancellation. **Working holiday** means a public holiday on which the Bank has declared that specific teams must work.

## 3. Eligibility for Transport
Employees of all grades are eligible for company transport only when they work an operations shift, that is a shift starting before 7:00 AM or ending after 8:00 PM. Employees working general shifts that start at or after 7:00 AM and end at or before 8:00 PM are not eligible, regardless of grade or the distance of their home from the office. There is no minimum distance requirement for eligible employees.

## 4. Shift Timings and Trip Windows
Operations shifts run from 6:00 AM to 3:00 PM, 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM and 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Pickups are planned to reach the office 15 minutes before shift start. The maximum planned ride time is 75 minutes. Drops depart within 20 minutes of shift end.

## 5. Booking and Cut-off Timings
Trip requests and changes for any day must be submitted in the MoveInSync app by 6:00 PM on the previous day. Requests for Monday must be submitted by 6:00 PM on the preceding Friday. Requests received after the cut-off are not rostered; the employee may apply for an ad-hoc trip under Section 11.

## 6. Roster Changes and Cancellations
Cancellations made before the 6:00 PM cut-off on the previous day carry no record. Same-day cancellations are permitted up to 1 hour before the scheduled pickup or drop and are recorded on the employee's trip history. A cancellation made less than 1 hour before the trip is treated as a no-show.

## 7. Night Drops — General Rules
Drops departing after 11:00 PM are night drops. During night drops the driver's live location is shared with the employee's registered emergency contact through the app. The cab must wait at the drop point until the employee confirms entry into their residence by app or phone call. A Transport Admin control room monitors all night drops.

## 8. Women's Safety and Security Escort
A trained security escort must be present in any cab carrying a woman employee on a drop departing after 10:00 PM, or on a pickup scheduled before 5:30 AM. The cab must not leave the office without the escort. Routing must be planned so that a woman employee is not dropped last wherever possible. The escort travels until the last passenger has been dropped.

## 9. Weekend Pickups
Kestrel Bank does not provide commute services on Saturdays or Sundays. Employees required to work on a weekend must make their own travel arrangements, and weekend travel costs are not reimbursed.

## 10. Holiday Pickups
Transport is provided on public holidays only if the day has been declared a working holiday for the employee's team, for example during year-end closing. The operations head must share the working-holiday roster with Transport Admin at least 3 working days in advance. Employees not on a declared working-holiday roster are not entitled to transport on public holidays.

## 11. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals
Ad-hoc trips are allowed only for business exigencies such as system outages, audits or regulatory deadlines. They must be requested in the app and approved by Transport Admin. The employee must state the business reason in the request. Ad-hoc trips for personal reasons are not permitted.

## 12. Ad-hoc Trip Cost Cap
The cost of a single ad-hoc trip is capped at ₹1,000. There is no provision to exceed the cap; any amount above ₹1,000 is borne by the employee and deducted from salary.

## 13. Airport Trips — Approval
Airport trips are provided only for official travel. Each airport trip must be approved by both Transport Admin and HR. Requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before the trip. An airport trip approved by only one of the two approvers will not be scheduled.

## 14. Airport Trips — Vehicle and Cost
All airport trips use a sedan, irrespective of grade. Waiting time of up to 45 minutes after scheduled landing is covered for arrivals. Parking and toll charges are paid by the company. Airport trips are not subject to the ad-hoc cost cap.

## 15. No-show Policy
A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board within the wait time in Section 16 without a prior cancellation. The first two no-shows in a calendar month are recorded but carry no penalty. From the third no-show onward in the same calendar month, ₹200 is deducted from the employee's salary for each no-show. No-show counts reset on the first day of each month.

## 16. Late Arrival and Wait Time
The driver waits 3 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. If the employee has not boarded by then, the cab departs. If a cab is more than 10 minutes late, the employee may report it in the app and Transport Admin will arrange a replacement.

## 17. Vehicle Standards
All cabs must be air-conditioned, fitted with a panic button and GPS device, and less than 4 years old. A sedan carries at most 4 employees. Drivers undergo police verification and a background check every year.

## 18. Code of Conduct in Vehicles
Employees must display their Kestrel ID card while boarding, wear seat belts and follow the driver's safety instructions. Smoking, alcohol and food are not permitted in cabs. Employees must not share their trip OTP with any other person. Breaches may result in suspension of commute services.

## 19. Data and Privacy
Trip data, including GPS traces and OTP logs, is retained for 90 days and then deleted, unless needed for an ongoing investigation. Trip data is accessible only to Transport Admin and, for safety investigations, to the Security team.

## 20. Grievances and Escalation
Complaints can be raised in the app or by email to Transport Admin, which responds within 1 working day. Unresolved complaints are escalated to the Head of Administration. Safety complaints are escalated to the Security team immediately.

## 21. Medical Emergencies
If an employee becomes unwell during a trip, the driver must stop safely, call 108 for an ambulance if required and inform the Transport Admin control room. The escort, if present, stays with the employee until help arrives.

## 22. Policy Governance
This policy is owned by the Head of Administration and approved by the Chief Human Resources Officer. It is reviewed annually or whenever regulations change. A new version supersedes all previous versions from its effective date.
