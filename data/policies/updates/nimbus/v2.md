# Nimbus Software — Employee Transport Policy (Version 2, effective 2026-07-01)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy sets out the rules for company-provided employee transport at Nimbus Software. It applies to all employees on the rolls of Nimbus Software working from the Bengaluru (Whitefield) and Pune (Hinjewadi) offices. Contractors, interns and consultants are not covered unless a section explicitly says so. Transport is operated through the MoveInSync platform and managed by the Transport Desk, which reports to the Administration function.

## 2. Definitions
**Transport Desk** means the team that plans rosters, assigns cabs and handles transport queries. **Roster** means the scheduled list of pickups and drops for a shift. **Pickup** is a trip from home to office; **drop** is a trip from office to home. **Ad-hoc trip** means any trip outside an employee's regular roster. **No-show** means an employee who does not board a rostered cab within the permitted wait time and has not cancelled. **Reporting manager** means the employee's direct line manager in the HR system. **Guest** means a person who is not a Nimbus employee and is visiting on official business.

## 3. Eligibility for Transport
Employees in grades L1 to L5 are eligible for rostered transport if their registered home address is more than 5 km by road from the office. Employees in grade L6 and above are not eligible for rostered cabs; they receive a monthly conveyance allowance under the Compensation Policy instead. Employees in grades L1 to L5 who live within 5 km of the office are eligible only for drops departing after 10:00 PM. The registered home address in the HR system is the only address used for eligibility; temporary addresses must be approved by the Transport Desk.

## 4. Shift Timings and Trip Windows
The general shift runs from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Support teams run shifts from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM and from 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM. A pickup will not be scheduled more than 90 minutes before shift start, and the maximum planned ride time for any employee is 90 minutes. Drops are scheduled within 30 minutes of shift end. Employees working outside these shifts must book an ad-hoc trip.

## 5. Booking and Cut-off Timings
Roster requests, including one-time changes to pickup or drop time, must be submitted in the MoveInSync app at least 3 hours before the trip. Requests submitted after the cut-off are not added to the roster and must be raised as ad-hoc trips under Section 11. Standing weekly rosters must be updated by Thursday 5:00 PM for the following week.

## 6. Roster Changes and Cancellations
An employee may cancel a rostered trip in the app up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup or drop time without any record. A cancellation made less than 2 hours before the trip is recorded as a late cancellation. Three late cancellations in a calendar month are counted as one no-show for the purposes of Section 16. Cancellations made through the Transport Desk by phone are treated the same as in-app cancellations.

## 7. Night Shift Drops — General Rules
Drops departing between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM are night drops. All night-drop cabs are GPS-tracked live by the Transport Desk. The driver must confirm each drop with the employee's trip OTP. After reaching home the employee must tap "Reached Safely" in the app; if this is not done within 15 minutes of the drop, the Transport Desk will call the employee. These rules apply to all employees regardless of gender.

## 8. Women's Safety and Security Escort
Any cab carrying a woman employee on a drop departing after 7:30 PM, or on a pickup scheduled before 6:00 AM, must have a trained security escort on board for the entire trip. If an escort is not available, the cab must not depart and the Transport Desk must arrange an escort or an alternative vehicle. Routing should avoid a woman employee being the first pickup or the last drop wherever possible. Women employees may request a woman escort, subject to availability.

## 9. Weekend Pickups
Transport on Saturday is available on request, provided the employee's reporting manager approves the request in the app by Friday 3:00 PM. There is no transport service on Sunday. Employees who are required to work on a Sunday must use their own transport and may claim reimbursement of up to ₹600 per day with reporting-manager approval.

## 10. Vehicle Standards
All cabs must be air-conditioned, less than 5 years old and carry a valid commercial permit. A sedan carries at most 3 employees and an SUV at most 5 employees. Drivers must have a police verification certificate renewed every 12 months. Vehicles failing an inspection are withdrawn until re-inspected.

## 11. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals
Ad-hoc trips are trips outside the regular roster, for example late working, early starts or official visits within the city. They must be requested in the app and approved by the employee's reporting manager. For urgent same-day trips the Transport Desk may approve the trip on the manager's behalf, and the reporting manager must ratify the approval by the next working day. Unratified trips are recovered from the employee.

## 12. Ad-hoc Trip Cost Cap
The cost of a single ad-hoc trip is capped at ₹1,500. A trip expected to exceed ₹1,500 requires prior approval from the employee's department head. Any amount above the cap that was not pre-approved is recovered from the employee's salary in the following payroll cycle.

## 13. Airport Trips — Approval
Airport pickups and drops are provided only for official travel. They must be requested at least 24 hours in advance and approved by the employee's reporting manager. Airport trips are available to employees of all grades, including L6 and above, because they are linked to business travel rather than daily commuting.

## 14. Airport Trips — Vehicle and Cost
Airport trips use a sedan for grades L1 to L4 and an SUV for grade L5 and above. For arrivals, waiting time of up to 60 minutes after the scheduled landing is covered by the company; waiting beyond 60 minutes requires the employee to inform the Transport Desk. Airport trips are not counted against the ad-hoc trip cost cap.

## 15. Guest Travel
Transport for guests may be arranged by a host employee by submitting a request to the Transport Desk at least 24 hours before the trip. The cost is charged to the host employee's cost centre. Guests must not be added to employee roster cabs and always travel in a separate vehicle. The host employee is responsible for sharing the guest's contact details with the Transport Desk.

## 16. No-show Policy
A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board a rostered cab within the wait time in Section 17 and has not cancelled the trip. Two no-shows in a calendar month result in the employee's transport being suspended for one calendar week. A third no-show in the same month results in suspension for the rest of that month, and the reporting manager is informed. No-show counts reset on the first day of each month.

## 17. Late Arrival and Wait Time
At pickup, the driver waits 5 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. If the employee has not boarded by then, the cab leaves and the employee must make their own arrangements. If a cab is more than 15 minutes late, the employee should raise a complaint in the app and may book an ad-hoc trip, which will be approved automatically.

## 18. Code of Conduct in Vehicles
Employees must carry their company ID card, wear seat belts and treat drivers and co-passengers with respect. Smoking, alcohol and loud phone calls are not permitted in cabs. Employees must not ask the driver to deviate from the planned route or make unscheduled stops. Misconduct may lead to withdrawal of transport and disciplinary action under the HR Code of Conduct.

## 19. Grievances and Escalation
Complaints about drivers, vehicles or trips can be raised in the app or with the Transport Desk. The Transport Desk responds within 4 working hours. Unresolved complaints can be escalated to the Administration Manager, who responds within 2 working days. Safety complaints are escalated immediately.

## 20. Medical Emergencies
If an employee falls ill during a trip, the driver must take the employee to the nearest hospital and call 112 if needed. The driver must inform the Transport Desk at once, and the Transport Desk informs HR and the employee's emergency contact. Other passengers are transferred to another cab.

## 21. Policy Governance
This policy is owned by the Administration function and reviewed at least twice a year. When a new version is published it replaces all earlier versions in full from its effective date. Questions about this policy should be directed to the Transport Desk.
