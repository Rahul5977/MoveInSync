# Saffron Pharma — Staff Transport Policy (Version 1, effective 2026-04-01)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy describes the staff transport service of Saffron Pharma for the Hyderabad manufacturing plant (Genome Valley) and the corporate office (Gachibowli). It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contract labour engaged through manpower agencies is covered only where a section explicitly says so. The service is run on the MoveInSync platform and managed by the Transport Cell under Facilities.

## 2. Definitions
**Transport Cell** means the Facilities team that manages rosters, vendors and transport queries. **Plant shift** means Shift A (6:00 AM to 2:00 PM), Shift B (2:00 PM to 10:00 PM) or Shift C (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM). **Weekend roster** means the published list of employees scheduled to work on Saturday or Sunday. **Visiting staff** means Saffron employees based at another Saffron site who are visiting Hyderabad on official duty. **Department head** means the head of the employee's department in the HR system.

## 3. Eligibility for Transport
Employees of any grade are eligible for transport if their registered home address is more than 8 km by road from their place of work. Production-floor employees working rotating plant shifts are eligible regardless of distance. Employees living 8 km or less from their place of work, other than plant-shift production staff, are not eligible.

## 4. Shift Timings and Trip Windows
Plant shifts are Shift A, Shift B and Shift C as defined in Section 2. The corporate office works from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Plant pickups are planned to arrive 20 minutes before shift start to allow for gowning and gate checks. The maximum planned ride time is 80 minutes.

## 5. Booking and Cut-off Timings
Trip requests and changes must be submitted in the MoveInSync app at least 2 hours before the trip. Requests made after the cut-off cannot be rostered and should be raised as ad-hoc trips under Section 10.

## 6. Roster Changes and Cancellations
Trips may be cancelled in the app at any time up to 30 minutes before the scheduled pickup or drop without penalty. Cancellations made less than 30 minutes before the trip are treated as no-shows under Section 15.

## 7. Night Shift Operations — General Rules
Shift C pickups and Shift B drops operate at night. All night trips are GPS-tracked by the Transport Cell, and every cab carries a first-aid kit. Night cabs for Shift C stop only at designated pickup points approved by the Transport Cell, not at individual doorsteps.

## 8. Women's Safety and Security Escort
Any cab carrying a woman employee on a drop departing after 9:00 PM, or on a pickup before 6:00 AM, must carry a trained security escort. Women employees on night trips are dropped at their doorstep, notwithstanding Section 7. The cab must wait until the employee has entered her residence. If no escort is available, the trip must not start.

## 9. Weekend Pickups
Transport on Saturdays and Sundays is provided to employees on the published weekend roster. Employees not on the weekend roster are not eligible for weekend transport. The weekend roster is published by the Transport Cell every Thursday.

## 10. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals
Ad-hoc trips cover travel outside the employee's roster, such as extended shifts, batch-release work or official visits within Hyderabad. Each ad-hoc trip must be requested in the app and approved by the employee's department head before the trip. ## 11. Ad-hoc Trip Cost Cap
The cost of a single ad-hoc trip is capped at ₹2,000. Trips expected to exceed ₹2,000 require written approval from the Plant Head or, for corporate staff, the Head of Facilities. Unapproved excess is recovered from the employee.

## 12. Airport Trips — Approval
Airport pickups and drops are provided for official travel and must be approved by the employee's department head at least 12 hours before the trip. Visiting staff arriving by air are covered under Section 14.

## 13. Airport Trips — Vehicle and Cost
Airport trips use a sedan for all employees, or an SUV when three or more employees travel together. Waiting time of up to 90 minutes after scheduled landing is covered for arrivals. Airport trips are not counted against the ad-hoc cost cap.

## 14. Guest and Visiting Staff Travel
Transport is provided for visiting staff from other Saffron sites for the duration of their official visit, including airport transfers, when requested by the host department. External guests, vendors, auditors and customers are not provided company transport under this policy; host departments must arrange their travel separately.

## 15. No-show Policy
A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board within the wait time in Section 16 without a timely cancellation. Two no-shows in a calendar month result in a written warning. A third no-show in the same month results in removal from the transport roster for the rest of that month. No-show counts reset at the start of each month.

## 16. Late Arrival and Wait Time
The driver waits 7 minutes after the scheduled pickup time at plant-shift pickup points and 5 minutes at other pickups. If a cab is more than 20 minutes late for a plant shift, the shift supervisor is informed so that the employee is not marked late.

## 17. Cab Access to Plant Premises
Cabs enter the plant only through Gate 2 and stop at the transport bay. Employees must not carry food, medicines or personal chemicals from the cab into the production block. Drivers are not permitted beyond the transport bay.

## 18. Vehicle Standards
All cabs must be air-conditioned, less than 6 years old, fitted with GPS and carry a first-aid kit. A sedan carries at most 4 employees and a tempo traveller at most 12 employees. Drivers are police-verified annually.

## 19. Code of Conduct in Vehicles
Employees must carry their Saffron ID card and wear seat belts. Smoking, alcohol and the use of speakerphones are not permitted. Employees must board and alight only at approved points. Repeated misconduct may result in withdrawal of transport.

## 20. Grievances and Escalation
Complaints can be raised in the app or at the Transport Cell help desk, which responds within 8 working hours. Unresolved complaints are escalated to the Head of Facilities. Safety complaints are escalated immediately to the Plant Security Officer.

## 21. Medical Emergencies
If an employee falls ill during a trip, the driver must take the employee to the nearest hospital or, for plant trips, to the plant Occupational Health Centre if it is closer. The driver must inform the Transport Cell immediately.

## 22. Policy Governance
This policy is owned by the Head of Facilities and reviewed annually. A new version replaces all earlier versions in full from its effective date.
